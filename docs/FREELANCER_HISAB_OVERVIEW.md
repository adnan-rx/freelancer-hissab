# FreelancerHisab — Platform Overview

## What is FreelancerHisab?
**FreelancerHisab** is a tailored financial operating system designed specifically for Pakistani freelancers, agencies, and software exporters. 

While generic accounting tools (like QuickBooks or Xero) can be overly complex and lack local context, FreelancerHisab is built ground-up to handle the unique realities of the Pakistani IT export ecosystem—specifically multi-currency income, Upwork/Fiverr fee structures, State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) remittance tracking, and FBR Section 154A tax laws.

---

## Core Value Proposition
*   **Zero Manual Entry:** Instantly parses Upwork/Fiverr CSV statements to automatically categorize income and platform fees.
*   **Automated Tax Compliance:** Calculates exact FBR tax liabilities based on your PSEB registration status (0.25% vs 1.0% export tax).
*   **Audit Readiness:** Tracks Proceeds Realization Certificates (PRCs) and SBP purpose codes, creating an Evidence Vault to defend against future FBR audits.
*   **Professional Invoicing:** Creates sleek, localized invoices that automatically include your IBAN and required SBP remittance instructions for foreign clients.

---

## Main Features

1.  **Smart Dashboard & Unified Ledger**
    *   Get a real-time pulse on your financial health (Income, Expenses, Net Savings, and pending Tax Liabilities).
    *   Track every transaction across multiple currencies with live Exchange Rate API integration.
2.  **Client & Invoice Management**
    *   Maintain a directory of local and foreign clients.
    *   Generate and export professional PDF invoices instantly.
    *   Track outstanding balances and mark invoices as paid to automatically update your ledger.
3.  **One-Click CSV Automation**
    *   Drag and drop platform statements (Upwork/Fiverr). The system automatically separates your net earnings from the platform commission fees.
4.  **FBR Tax Engine & Filing Simulator**
    *   Real-time tax calculation engine aware of the latest Income Tax Ordinance 2001 laws for IT exporters.
    *   Simulate your tax return before filing to ensure you have the correct documentation and PRCs.
5.  **Wealth Reconciliation (Coming Soon)**
    *   Track multi-currency assets (e.g., Meezan Bank, Payoneer, Binance) and reconcile your net worth against your reported income.

## The Key User Workflow (The Journey)

Here is how a freelancer typically uses the app:

1.  **Onboard:** The user signs up and adds their PSEB number and Bank IBAN in settings.
2.  **Import:** At the end of the month, they upload their Upwork CSV statement. The app automatically logs the income and the Upwork service fees.
3.  **Bill Clients:** For direct clients, they generate a PDF Invoice inside the app and send it off.
4.  **Track Remittances:** When foreign funds hit their bank, they update the income record with the SBP Purpose Code (9151) and the PRC reference number provided by the bank.
5.  **Calculate Tax:** They open the Tax Simulator, which instantly proves that because they logged their PRCs and are PSEB registered, they only owe 0.25% tax.
6.  **Report:** They generate a Profit & Loss report to see exactly how much their business grew that month.
