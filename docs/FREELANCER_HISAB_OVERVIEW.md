# FreelancerHisab: The Complete Financial System for Pakistani IT Exporters

## 🌟 Executive Summary

FreelancerHisab is a financial management system made specially for Pakistani freelancers, software agencies, and IT exporters.

Normal accounting tools like QuickBooks or Xero do not fully solve the specific problems Pakistani IT exporters face. These include **State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) remittance tracking, PSEB registration benefits, and Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) Section 154A tax rules**.

FreelancerHisab solves these problems in one place. It helps automate financial tracking, keeps records ready for audits, and calculates tax liabilities in real time. This allows IT professionals to focus on their work instead of spending hours on complicated bookkeeping.

---

## 🎯 The Core Problems We Solve

Pakistani freelancers have to deal with several financial and tax-related challenges:

1. **Platform Fees:** Upwork and Fiverr take commissions from freelancer earnings. Because of this, it can be difficult and time-consuming to manually track actual income and platform expenses.

2. **SBP Compliance:** To claim IT export tax benefits, freelancers need to properly track their **Proceeds Realization Certificates (PRCs)** and make sure banks use the correct **SBP Purpose Codes** (for example, 9151 for Software).

3. **FBR Tax Rules (Section 154A):** IT export income is taxed at a lower rate of **0.25% for PSEB-registered exporters** or **1.0% for unregistered exporters**, while local income is taxed using the normal, much higher tax slab rates.

4. **Wealth Reconciliation:** Every year, the FBR requires your declared wealth to properly match your reported income and expenses.

**FreelancerHisab brings all of these things together and automates the process in one platform.**

## 🚀 Main Features

### 1. FBR Tax Engine & Filing Simulator (Section 154A)

* **Real-Time Tax Calculation:** The built-in tax engine is programmed according to the Income Tax Ordinance 2001. It separates your income into **Foreign IT Export** and **Local Income**, then applies the correct **0.25%, 1.0%, or standard tax slab rates**.

* **PSEB Savings Tracker:** The system instantly shows how much tax you can save by keeping an active **Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB)** registration.

* **Filing Readiness Score (Can-I-File):** The system checks your financial records before you file your tax return. For example, if you have foreign income but no linked PRC reference, it will flag this as a compliance risk. This helps you find problems before submitting your return.

---

### 2. SBP PRC & Remittance Tracking

* **Purpose Code Tagging:** Every income entry allows you to add the correct **SBP Purpose Code** (such as 9100 or 9151) and the **PRC Reference Number** provided by your bank.

* **Evidence Vault:** A secure digital storage area where you can upload PRC PDF files and link them directly to the related income transactions. This keeps your records organized and ready for an FBR audit.

---

### 3. One-Click Platform CSV Automation

* **No Manual Data Entry:** Simply drag and drop your Upwork or Fiverr CSV statement into the app.

* **Smart Data Processing:** The system automatically identifies your **gross earnings** and records them as income. It also identifies platform commissions and records them as **tax-deductible business expenses**.

* **Automatic Client Creation:** When the system finds a new client in your CSV file, it can automatically create that client in your client directory.

---

### 4. Multi-Currency Ledger & Live Exchange Rates

* **Live Exchange Rates:** The system connects with the live ExchangeRate-API. For example, when you record a **$2,000 USD payment**, the system automatically gets the current USD-to-PKR exchange rate and records the PKR value.

* **Expense Tracking:** You can record normal business expenses such as internet bills, software subscriptions, and co-working spaces. This helps you maintain an accurate **Profit & Loss (P&L)** statement and track your tax-deductible business expenses.

---

### 5. Professional & Localized Invoicing

* **Professional PDF Invoices:** Create clean and professional invoices instantly in different currencies.

* **Built-in SBP Instructions:** Your invoices automatically include your **IBAN** and important remittance instructions, such as *"Please remit under SBP Purpose Code 9151."* This helps your foreign clients send payments using the correct purpose code.

* **Payment Status Tracking:** When an invoice is marked as paid, the revenue can automatically be added to your income ledger.

---

### 6. Wealth Management & Reconciliation

* **Track Multiple Assets:** Keep track of your **Meezan Bank PKR balance, Payoneer USD balance, and Binance Crypto balance** from one dashboard.

* **Net Worth Calculator:** The system automatically converts your foreign balances into PKR so you can see your total real-time net worth.

* **Reconciliation Check:** The system checks that your:

  **Opening Wealth + Net Profit = Closing Wealth**

  This helps make sure your numbers match when preparing your FBR wealth statement.

---

### 7. Financial Analytics & Reporting

* **Dashboard KPIs:** See important numbers in real time, including **Total Income, Total Expenses, Net Savings, and Pending Invoices**.

* **Income Breakdown:** Visual charts show where your income is coming from, such as **Upwork, Fiverr, or Direct Clients**.

* **Exportable Reports:** Generate detailed **CSV and PDF financial reports** that can be shared with your accountant or used when preparing your FBR IRIS filing.

## 📈 How the User Journey Works

### 1. Onboarding

The freelancer creates their profile and securely adds information such as their **Bank IBAN and PSEB registration number**.

### 2. Automated Data Import

At the end of the month, the freelancer uploads their Upwork CSV file. Hundreds of transactions and platform fees are automatically processed and categorized.

### 3. Direct Invoicing

For a direct US client, the freelancer creates a professional PDF invoice. The invoice automatically includes the required **SBP remittance instructions**.

### 4. Compliance Tracking

When the payment reaches the freelancer's local bank account, they add the **PRC reference number** to the related income record.

### 5. Tax Season Check

When tax season arrives, the freelancer opens the **Tax Simulator**.

The system checks that:

* Foreign income has the required PRC records.
* The correct **0.25% PSEB tax rate** is applied.
* Wealth is properly reconciled.
* Financial records are complete.

The system then provides the freelancer with the numbers and information they need to prepare their FBR filing.
